🌸 Seasons of Japan

Website Planning Document

Site Name

Seasons of Japan

The name "Seasons of Japan" represents a website about the different seasons in Japan and the activities people can enjoy throughout the year. I chose this name because it is simple, easy to remember, and clearly describes the purpose of the website.

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to provide simple and useful information about seasonal activities in Japan. The website will introduce activities that people can enjoy during spring, summer, autumn, and winter. Visitors will be able to learn about different activities and choose their favorite seasonal activity.

Scenarios

  • What activities can I enjoy in Japan during each season?
  • Which season has activities that I might enjoy with my family or friends?
  • How can I choose my favorite seasonal activity?

Color Schema

The website will use soft and natural colors inspired by the beauty of Japan's seasons.

Sakura Pink #E8A0BF
Dark Navy #26354A
Light Cream #FFF8F0

Sakura Pink: Used for headings, buttons, accents, and decorative elements.

Dark Navy: Used for navigation, main text, headings, and the footer.

Light Cream: Used as the main background color.

Typography

Poppins - Headings

Poppins will be used for the main headings and navigation. It provides a clean and modern appearance.

Open Sans - Body Text

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs and other body content because it is clear and easy to read on mobile and desktop screens.
